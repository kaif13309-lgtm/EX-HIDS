How the hybrid works

Network + host event→Signature rules+Anomaly model (z-score)→Fusion→Explanation

Signature rules catch known attack patterns with named reasons. The anomaly model compares each feature to a learned normal baseline and catches unknown behaviour. Fusion combines both scores, and each feature’s share of the anomaly score is reported as its contribution, similar to SHAP-style attribution.
